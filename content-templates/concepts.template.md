## Callout blocks (optional)

Use GitHub-alert syntax for emphasis boxes. EXACTLY these 4 types are supported
(case-sensitive, marker on its own first line of the blockquote):

```md
> [!TIP]
> A concise, actionable pointer.

> [!WARNING]
> A common pitfall or gotcha interviewers probe.

> [!INTERVIEW]
> How this shows up in interviews / how to answer it.

> [!KEY-TAKEAWAY]
> The one thing to remember.
```

The box body may contain normal markdown (lists, `code`, **bold**). Plain
blockquotes without a `[!TYPE]` marker still render as ordinary quotes. Don't
overuse — 1–3 per topic.
